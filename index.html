Inclusive bridge redesign

02

Car infotainment redesign

Redesigned the UI of a car infotainment system in Figma to reduce driver cognitive load and eye fixation time. The process included photo journaling, affinity diagrams, paper prototyping, 7-task usability tests, and a final high-fidelity Figma prototype covering radio, Bluetooth, and navigation.

Figma Usability testing Cognitive design Automotive UX

03

Hot Potato — campfire safety device

Designed and built an Arduino-based prototype to help campers maintain fire safety overnight. The device detects dangerous heat spikes, a dying flame, CO levels in tents, wind direction, and nearby obstacles — alerting users even when away from camp via portable warning units.

Arduino Physical prototype Calm technology Safety design

About me

Designing for the
way people actually live

I'm a UX designer based in Sweden, currently completing a Master's in Human-Computer Interaction at Umeå University. I work across the full design process — from user research and ideation to prototyping and testing — with a focus on designing for real human contexts, whether that's urban infrastructure, vehicles, or the outdoors.

I've gained industry exposure through production work at Volvo Cars, where I got to see how design decisions play out at scale.

Methods

Usability testing, user interviews, affinity diagrams, paper prototyping, cognitive walkthroughs, sketching

Tools

Figma, Arduino, Processing, pen & paper

Education
