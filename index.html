03 / Approach

Start with the situation,
not the interface.

I am interested in design problems where context matters. A bridge is not just a structure. An infotainment system is not just a screen. A campfire product is not just a sensor.

Across these projects, I work from the conditions around the interaction: accessibility, attention, physical behaviour and the consequences of getting the experience wrong.

04 / About

Elias Bergman

UX designer with an interest in systems that meet people where they are.

My work so far spans inclusive design, automotive interaction and physical prototyping. I like projects where research, design and making have to work together rather than living in separate phases.

This portfolio is intentionally simple. The projects should do the talking.
