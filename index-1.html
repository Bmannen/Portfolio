About me

Designing for the
way people actually live

I'm a UX designer based in Sweden, currently completing a Master's in Human-Computer Interaction at Umeå University. I work across the full design process — from user research and ideation to prototyping and testing — with a focus on designing for real human contexts.

I've gained industry exposure through production work at Volvo Cars, where I got to see how design decisions play out at scale.

Methods

Usability testing, user interviews, affinity diagrams, paper prototyping, cognitive walkthroughs, sketching

Tools

Figma, Arduino, Processing, pen & paper

Education

MSc Human-Computer Interaction, Umeå University
BSc Cognitive Science, Umeå University
